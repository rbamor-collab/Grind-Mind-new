Sales Data Analysis & Report

Imagine that you are a data analyst assigned to prepare a formal sales performance report for management. Apply all advanced Excel techniques from Course 2 to organize, process, and visualize your dataset.

📋 Activity Requirements & Course 2 Integration:

  • Worksheet Setup & Data Entry: Copy the raw sales table below into a new Excel worksheet and format headers cleanly.
  • Formulas & Calculations: Calculate Total Sales per row (= Quantity * Unit Price) and compute grand totals using =SUM().
  • Sorting & Filtering (Lesson 3.1): Sort the dataset by Total Sales from highest to lowest, and apply a filter to isolate transactions above ₱10,000.
  • Conditional Formatting (Lesson 3.1): Highlight top sales figures (Total Sales > ₱30,000) in soft green and highlight low quantities (< 10) in light red.
  • Data Visualization (Lesson 3.2): Insert a Bar Chart or Column Chart comparing Total Sales across different Regions or Categories. Add clear titles and data labels.

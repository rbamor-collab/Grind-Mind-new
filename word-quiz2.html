Formatting an Official Corporate Memorandum

Apply what you have learned in Module 2 by formatting the raw memorandum text and schedule data provided below into a professional, standardized corporate document in MS Word.

📋 Challenge Requirements:

  • Memo Header Layout: Properly structure the MEMORANDUM header (TO, FROM, DATE, SUBJECT) with clean alignment and a dividing line.
  • Insert & Format Tables: Organize the meeting schedule into a clean table with appropriate borders, cell padding, and header shading.
  • Margins & Spacing: Set standard margins (1 inch) and maintain professional line spacing (1.15 or 1.5).
  • Heading Hierarchy: Use bold, professional fonts (e.g., Arial or Calibri) for section headings.
  • Final Polish: Ensure proper alignment, professional tone, and clean document flow.

📄 Raw Data to Copy:

TO: All Administrative Division Personnel FROM: Office of the Director DATE: October 29, 2026 SUBJECT: Implementation of Standardized Digital Workflow and Quarterly Review 1. Purpose This memorandum serves to formally announce the rollout of our new digital workflow templates across all departments to reduce document processing bottlenecks and streamline daily operations. 2. Implementation Schedule Nov 03, 2026 | Finance Division | Word Document Standardization Nov 10, 2026 | Human Resources | Excel Data Tracking & Formulas Nov 17, 2026 | Operations Team | PowerPoint Presentation Pitch 3. Action Required All department heads are required to attend the orientation session and submit their feedback reports on or before November 25, 2026. Your cooperation is highly appreciated.
